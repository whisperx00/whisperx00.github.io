6+ years in offensive security, with depth in Linux-kernel vulnerability research, hypervisor escapes, and adversary-emulation engagements. Currently a Senior Offensive Security Researcher at Praetorian Labs (Berlin), where I lead the kernel-fuzzing track and mentor two junior researchers.

Track record: 7 CVEs assigned across the Linux kernel, QEMU, Xen and V8 (avg CVSS 7.9, range 5.4–9.8); USENIX Security '26 distinguished paper on eBPF verifier fuzzing; $184k+ in coordinated bug-bounty payouts from Google, Mozilla, and two undisclosed vendors. Comfortable in C, Rust, Python, Go and a regrettable amount of bash; equally happy in front of GDB, IDA, or a whiteboard.

What I'm looking for: a senior IC or research-lead role at a security-focused team that ships, with budget for original VR (not just consulting hours). Open to product security at scale (browser/kernel/OS), red-team capability development, or staying in academic-adjacent VR. EU/UK/CH base preferred; senior remote with quarterly co-location works too.
