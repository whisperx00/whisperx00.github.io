paper · usenix security 2026

Structure-aware grammar fuzzing of the Linux eBPF verifier

We present a grammar-based, coverage-guided fuzzer for the eBPF verifier's state-machine transitions, discovering three memory-safety bugs (two CVEs, one embargoed) in kernels 6.2–6.8. Our approach models the verifier's register-type lattice as a context-free grammar and uses typed mutations to stay on the in-distribution side of valid programs while still exercising transition edges rarely hit by libfuzzer.

N. Aren, H. van Wees, P. Okonkwo — TU Delft · Systems Security Group

▸disclosure / responsible & coordinated

pgp required for sensitive reports

disclosure policy vendor & research

i follow a coordinated vulnerability disclosure process with a default 90-day embargo, extendable once by 30 days with a written timeline. public release happens at min(patch_ship_date, embargo_end).

  1. initial report — pgp-encrypted to 0xA91F3C2D, includes PoC and affected versions.
  2. triage window — 7 days for vendor to acknowledge and assign a tracker.
  3. fix window — 90 days default. if vendor is responsive and has a credible timeline, i'll extend.
  4. public writeup — published here after patch ships or embargo ends, whichever is first.

credits: cveadvisory htmlacks in release notes accepted. thanks requested only where it's accurate.
